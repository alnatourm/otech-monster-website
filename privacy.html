Last updated: November 19, 2026 | Company: OTECH, Amman, Jordan | Contact: support@otech.monster | WhatsApp: +962 7 7680 1240

TL;DR: OTECH BOT is 100% local. Your customer chats, WhatsApp messages, and business data stay on YOUR PC. We do not upload them to our cloud. We do not sell them. We do not read them.

1. What OTECH BOT Does

OTECH BOT runs AI bots (Sales, Support, Booking, Data) directly on your Windows PC using a local AI engine (Ollama). The AI processes your messages locally, on your device.

2. What We Collect

  • We DO NOT collect: Your WhatsApp chats, customer names, customer phone numbers, customer messages, sales data, or business data.
  • We DO collect for license activation only: Your license key (e.g. OTECH-JO-...), your PC hardware ID (to prevent key sharing), app version, and activation date/time. This is sent to license.otech.monster only to validate your key.
  • Website: otech.monster uses Vercel Analytics (anonymous visits, no personal data). No cookies for tracking.

3. What We Never Do

  • Never upload your chats to cloud
  • Never sell your data
  • Never train AI on your data
  • Never share your data with third parties, except as required by law in Jordan

4. WhatsApp

When you connect WhatsApp, OTECH BOT uses WhatsApp on your PC to send/reply. WhatsApp messages are handled by WhatsApp / Meta under their own policy. OTECH does not store a copy on our servers.

5. Your Rights

You can delete all OTECH data anytime by uninstalling the app - all local data is deleted with it. To delete your license record, email support@otech.monster.

6. Children

OTECH BOT is for businesses (18+). Not for children.

7. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above.
